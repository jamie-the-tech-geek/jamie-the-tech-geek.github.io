Privacy Policy for Earbud Man - Find my Airpods

Effective Date: January 11, 2026
Last Updated: August 22, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Earbud Man - Find my Airpods. We value your privacy and are committed to being transparent about how our app functions and what data it uses.

2. Data Collection

We do not directly collect, store, or request any personal information such as your name, email address, or location history. However, our app displays advertisements through Google AdMob, which may collect certain data (see Section 4).

3. Permissions Used

To function correctly, Earbud Man requires the following permissions:

  • Bluetooth Connect: Used solely to detect the connection status and battery level of your wireless earbuds. We do not track your devices or store their unique IDs.
  • Camera (Flashlight): Used solely to provide the "Torch" feature to help you find lost items in dark areas. We do not access the camera feed, take photos, or record video.

4. Advertising

This app uses Google AdMob to display ads, which helps keep the app free to use. AdMob may collect data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, and general app usage information in order to serve ads and measure their performance. This data is processed according to Google's own privacy and advertising policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps

You can control ad personalization for your device at any time through your Google Account's Ads Settings.

5. In-App Purchases

This app offers an optional one-time premium purchase processed through Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment details — these are handled entirely by Google Play.

6. Third-Party Sharing

Aside from the advertising data described in Section 4, which is processed by Google/AdMob to serve ads, we do not sell, trade, or share any data with third parties.

7. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Advertising shown in this app is served in accordance with Google Play's policies for apps not primarily directed at children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
