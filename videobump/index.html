Privacy Policy for VideoBump

Effective Date: August 23, 2026
Last Updated: August 23, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to VideoBump. We value your privacy and are committed to being transparent about how our app functions and what data it uses.

2. Data Collection

VideoBump does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The app does not use analytics, does not display advertisements, and has no server component — everything it does happens entirely on your device.

3. How VideoBump Accesses Your Videos

VideoBump does not request broad access to your video library. It only ever receives the specific video (or videos) you choose to share into it using Android's built-in Share feature. When you use the "Move" option, Android shows you a one-time system permission prompt asking you to confirm write access to that specific video before its date is changed — VideoBump never accesses or modifies any video you haven't explicitly shared with it.

A small record of a video's original date is kept in the app's private storage on your device only, so that the "Undo" feature (including "Undo all" for a batch) can restore it later. This record never leaves your device.

4. In-App Purchases

This app offers an optional one-time purchase to unlock batch mode, processed through Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment details — these are handled entirely by Google Play. See Google's own policies for how they handle this data:

5. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share any data with third parties. This app does not use any advertising or analytics SDKs.

6. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, regardless of age.
