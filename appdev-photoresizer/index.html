Privacy Policy for App Dev - Photo Resizer

Effective Date: September 13, 2026
Last Updated: September 13, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to App Dev - Photo Resizer. We value your privacy and are committed to being transparent about how our app functions and what data it uses.

2. Data Collection

App Dev - Photo Resizer does not directly collect, store, or transmit any personal information, does not use analytics, and has no server component. All photo cropping and resizing happens entirely on your device. However, the app displays advertisements through Google AdMob, which may collect certain data (see Section 4).

3. How the App Accesses Your Photos

App Dev - Photo Resizer does not request broad access to your photo library. It only ever receives the specific photo (or photos) you choose using Android's built-in photo picker or file picker. Cropped and resized images are saved either to your device's Downloads folder or shared directly through Android's built-in Share feature, at your request — the app never accesses, uploads, or transmits your photos anywhere else.

4. Advertising

This app uses Google AdMob to display ads (including banner and interstitial ads), which helps keep the app free to use. AdMob may collect data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, and general app usage information in order to serve ads and measure their performance. This data is processed according to Google's own privacy and advertising policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps

You can control ad personalization for your device at any time through your Google Account's Ads Settings.

5. Third-Party Sharing

Aside from the advertising data described in Section 4, which is processed by Google/AdMob to serve ads, we do not sell, trade, or share any data with third parties.

6. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, regardless of age. Advertising shown in this app is served in accordance with Google Play's policies for apps not primarily directed at children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
